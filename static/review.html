About these scores

Accuracy estimates use the mean of 100 × exp(−winning-chance loss / 20) for each player’s moves. Winning chances use a logistic conversion of Stockfish’s score. An inaccuracy loses at least 5 percentage points, a mistake 10, and a blunder 20. The engine’s first choice is labeled Best; other moves losing under 2 points are Excellent, and under 5 are Good. All positions are included.

Brilliant (!!) estimates a sound sacrifice: a new offer of a minor piece or more, a near-best move, a non-losing position and a separate engine search of the capture. Great (!) requires the best move and a same-depth second engine line losing at least 10 winning-chance points; forced moves and already decisive positions are excluded. No label is guaranteed to appear in every game.

This is our own estimate, not Chess.com’s CAPS2 accuracy or its move-label system. Short engine searches can miss tactics; labels and suggested lines can change with deeper analysis. No rating is inferred. Feedback describes engine results rather than claiming a human coach’s explanation.
