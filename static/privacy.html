Privacy & disclosures

Updated October 22, 2026

Public game data

When you analyze a username, your browser requests public games directly from Lichess or Chess.com. Those services receive the username requested, your IP address and standard request information. Stockfish evaluates the games on your device. We do not run an analysis server or ask for chess account passwords.

Saved on your device

This app stores up to five recent analysis summaries in this browser’s local storage: username, site, sample size, time control, analysis date and mistakes per game. Select Clear history on the home screen to remove them. Full game reports are not stored there. Downloads you save remain on your device until you delete them.

A service worker may cache the app’s files so the interface can load offline. It does not cache third-party game requests. You can remove site storage through your browser settings.

Hosting and optional analytics

The hosting provider receives normal website requests and may keep access logs under its own policies. Analytics are disabled in the default configuration. If the site owner enables Plausible, it receives page views and named interaction events, including selected chess service, sample size and resource theme. Chess usernames are not added to custom event properties. See Plausible’s data policy.

Payments, email and sponsors

Optional support and shop buttons open an external payment provider. This site never collects card details or confirms purchases. If an optional newsletter is enabled, its form submits the email you enter directly to that provider. Review the destination’s policies before using it.

Paid placements are labeled Sponsored. Training links marked as affiliate links may earn the owner a commission. Ordinary practice links are not paid endorsements. No third-party ad network is enabled by default.

Questions

For a purchase question, use the seller contact provided on the checkout page. For this app, use the project’s issue tracker. Avoid posting private information in public issues.
