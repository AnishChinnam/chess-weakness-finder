BEFORE YOU START

A few things to know

Is this free?

Yes. Analysis, your practice plan and saved summaries are free. Optional purchases or support links may be shown separately.

How accurate is the analysis?

Stockfish runs short searches on your device. Results can differ from deeper analysis, and theme labels are estimates. Move-number phases are a simple grouping, not a material-based definition. Use a larger sample before drawing conclusions.

Do I need to connect my account?

No. Enter a public Lichess or Chess.com username. We fetch public games directly from that service. Your password is never requested.

Can I install this like an app?

Yes, on supported browsers. Use Install app when offered, or your browser’s Add to Dock / Add to Home Screen option. Fetching new games requires internet access.
