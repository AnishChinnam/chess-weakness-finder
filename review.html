About these scores

Accuracy estimates use the mean of 100 × exp(−winning-chance loss / 20) for each player’s moves. Winning chances use a logistic conversion of Stockfish’s score. An inaccuracy loses at least 5 percentage points, a mistake 10, and a blunder 15. The engine’s first choice is labeled Best; other moves losing under 1 point are Excellent, and under 5 are Good. All positions are included.

This is our own estimate, not Chess.com’s CAPS2 accuracy or its move-label system. Short engine searches can miss tactics; labels and suggested lines can change with deeper analysis. No rating is inferred. Feedback describes engine results rather than claiming a human coach’s explanation.
