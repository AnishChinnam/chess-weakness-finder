Turn game review into a practice routine.

Chess Weakness Finder reviews your recent public blitz and rapid games, groups important mistakes, and suggests a week of focused practice. You can use it without signing in or connecting your chess account.

How mistakes are identified

Stockfish evaluates positions before and after each move. We convert those scores into a winning-chance estimate using a logistic model. Inaccuracies drop that estimate by at least 5 percentage points, mistakes by at least 10, and blunders by at least 15. Moves starting from −7 pawns or worse are excluded, as are moves matching the engine’s first choice.

What the labels mean

Hanging pieces and missed tactics are heuristic estimates. Opening, middlegame and endgame use move numbers 1–12, 13–30 and 31 onward. These labels do not prove why a move was wrong. Time trouble uses recorded clock values under 60 seconds after a move, so increment can affect the result.

A short search is a starting point

Analysis uses short searches to keep the app usable on everyday devices. Larger batches use less thinking time per position. Results may change with deeper analysis. Compare the same sample size and time control, and use multiple games rather than treating a single score as a rating or a coaching diagnosis.

A plan you can actually follow

The seven-day plan selects practical exercises based on your most common flagged themes. It is a rule-based routine, not a human coaching service. Your recent summaries stay in this browser so you can compare check-ins with fresh games.
